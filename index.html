Who We Are

A small crew. Real workmanship.

ALL Trades Construction & Handyman Services is owned and operated by Jack and Andrew — two Cleveland guys who got tired of seeing homeowners chase down contractors who don't return calls and don't finish the job.

We started this company on a simple idea: do good work, charge a fair price, show up when we say we will. Whether it's a 30-minute repair or a six-week remodel, you'll be talking to one of the owners — not a phone tree, not a sub we've never met.

From Lakewood to Strongsville to Mentor, we treat every home like it was our grandma's. That's the whole philosophy.

Frequently Asked

Common questions.

Are estimates really free? +
Yes. We'll come out, look at the job in person, and give you a clear written estimate at no cost. No pressure to book.
What areas do you serve? +
The whole Greater Cleveland area — including Lakewood, Parma, Strongsville, Westlake, Rocky River, Mentor, Brunswick, Medina, and everywhere in between. If you're not sure, just call and ask.
How quickly can you start? +
For small handyman jobs, often same week. For larger remodels, typically 1–3 weeks out depending on the season. We'll give you a real timeline at the estimate.
Do you take on small jobs? +
Yes — small jobs are half of what we do. A single door that won't close, a list of fixes around the house, a leaky faucet… nothing's too small.
Are you licensed and insured? +
Yes. We're a fully insured and bonded Ohio LLC. Happy to send proof of insurance with any estimate.
How do payments work? +
For small jobs, payment is due on completion. For larger projects, we use a simple deposit + milestone schedule we'll lay out before any work starts. We also accept credit and debit cards.
Do you offer discounts? +
Yes — 10% off for first-time customers, seniors, and veterans. Just mention it when you book your estimate.
Don't see your question?

Just ask. Either owner will get back to you the same day — usually within a couple of hours.
